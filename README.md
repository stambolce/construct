## 🇬🇧 EN — Construction Management Software Built for Real Operations

**CONSTRUCT OS** is a construction operations software platform being developed by **[PROCESS IN](https://processin.mk)**. Its goal is to bring construction project execution, materials, purchasing, subcontractors, resources, cost tracking and management visibility into one coordinated workspace.

Construction companies often coordinate work across disconnected spreadsheets, messaging apps and paper documents. CONSTRUCT OS is designed around the relationships that matter: **a project, its phases, the people delivering it, the materials consumed, the costs incurred and the decisions that follow.**

### Core product pillars

| Pillar | Product direction |
|---|---|
| **Projects & Sites** | Project portfolio, construction sites, project stages, milestones, progress and delivery status |
| **Planning & Execution** | Tasks, schedules, dependencies, site activities, deadlines and work tracking |
| **Materials & Procurement** | Material catalogs, quantities, requirements, suppliers, purchase workflows and material cost visibility |
| **Subcontractors & Teams** | Contractors, teams, responsibilities, work assignments and execution records |
| **Resources & Equipment** | Equipment, machinery and resource allocation by project |
| **Budgeting & Cost Control** | Planned versus actual costs, expense categories, commitments, budget changes and variance monitoring |
| **Project Financial Visibility** | Project profitability indicators, cost-to-complete views and management reporting |
| **Documents & Approvals** | Project files, evidence, change requests, approvals and traceable decisions |
| **Dashboards & Analytics** | Portfolio overview, cost trends, schedule risks and operational KPIs |
| **Customer & Partner Relationships** | Project-linked contacts, suppliers, clients and supporting CRM workflows |
| **Property Sales — Optional Module** | Units such as apartments, commercial premises and shops, availability, sales pipeline and reservations |
| **Multi-tenant SaaS Foundation** | Organization-level separation, user roles, permissions, plan entitlements and subscription billing |

These are **product architecture and planned capabilities**, not a declaration that each module is already live.

### The construction workflow

**Plan → Procure → Execute → Track → Control → Deliver**

1. Create a construction project with scope, phases, people and target budget.
2. Plan tasks, milestones, material needs and procurement.
3. Coordinate work performed by internal teams and subcontractors.
4. Record material usage, commitments and project expenses.
5. Compare progress and actual cost against the plan.
6. Review exceptions, approve changes and prepare project handover.

### Built for

- Construction companies and general contractors
- Project managers and site managers
- Property developers and investors
- Subcontractor coordination teams
- Businesses managing multiple simultaneous construction projects

### Product boundaries

CONSTRUCT OS is **construction-first**: project delivery and operational cost control are the core. Property sales is an **optional supporting module**. The product is **not positioned as a replacement for full statutory accounting or payroll software**.

## 🇲🇰 MK — Софтвер за управување со градежни проекти и трошоци

**CONSTRUCT OS** е платформа во развој од **[PROCESS IN](https://processin.mk)**, наменета за градежни компании, инвеститори, раководители на проекти и тимови на градилишта.

Целта е сите важни информации за градежниот проект да бидат поврзани: **планирање, фази, задачи, материјали, набавки, добавувачи, подизведувачи, ресурси, буџети, реални трошоци и напредок на изведбата.**

**Ова е функционална визија на производот.** Не значи дека сите модули се завршени или јавно достапни.

### Каков проблем решава?

Кога набавките се во една табела, трошоците во друга, задачите во пораки, а документацијата во папки, раководителите тешко добиваат точна слика за проектот.

CONSTRUCT OS е замислен да одговори на практични прашања: **Што е планирано? Што е завршено? Кои материјали се потребни? Колку е потрошено? Каде има отстапување? Што треба да се преземе следно?**

### За кого е наменет?

За градежни компании, инвеститори, проектни менаџери, раководители на градилишта и компании што управуваат со повеќе проекти.

**Фокусот е градежна оперативност и контрола на проекти.** Сметководство и пресметка на плати не се основен дел од производот. Продажбата на станови и деловни простории е изборен модул.
